## **Base Vials**

*These vials form the basic building blocks of demon alchemy.*

**Blood Sample**  
*Recipe: Minced Human Flesh \+ Empty Vial in an Alchemy Table*

**Rotten Blood Sample**  
Can be used instead of a blood sample, but with a chance of failure  
*Recipe: Rotten Flesh;+ Empty Vial in an Alchemy Table*

**Crude Vial**  
*Recipe: Hemolith Dust \+ Blood Sample in a Brewing Stand*

**Refined Vial**  
Used for making amplifiers   
*Recipe: Fermented Orchid \+ Crude Vial in a Brewing Stand*

**Cruel Vial**  
Used for making blood demon art catalysts  
*Recipe: Immortal Daisy \+ Crude Vial in a Brewing Stand*

## **Amplifier Vials** 

*Amplifiers can be used directly in the Blood Demon Art crafter to upgrade potion effects and blood demon art forms. Amplifiers are created in an Alchemy Table by placing a culture in the top slot and a refined vial in the bottom slot.*

## **Blood Demon Art Catalysts**

*Blood Demon Art Catalysts can be used to unlock new moves for your blood demon art forms.*  
*Catalysts are made with an extract and a Cruel Vial*  
*Catalysts can be combined with extracts or catalysts to make new catalysts*  
*In the recipes for making catalysts, the catalyst in the top slot (ingredient slot) can be either a catalyst or an extract, just the bottom slot needs to be either a catalyst or a cruel vial. In the ingredient slot, catalysts and extracts of the same type (ie. Wither Catalyst vs Wither Extract) are interchangeable. When making catalysts from other catalysts, the ingredients can be placed in either order (for example Vex Catalyst can be made with Illager’s Extract and Azure Catalyst, or Azure Extract and Illager’s Catalyst, or Azure Catalyst and Illager’s Catalyst).*

**Wither Catalyst**
