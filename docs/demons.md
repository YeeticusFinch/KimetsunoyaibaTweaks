## Progression Pattern

The numbered demons appear to follow a progression pattern where:
- Even-numbered demons tend to have higher health or special attributes
- Odd-numbered demons are closer to the base demon stats
- Demon6 stands out as having significantly higher stats, especially health (250.0) and armor toughness (6.0)
- Demon8 also has enhanced stats with high attack damage (6.0) and armor toughness (4.0)
- All numbered demons share identical armor (16.0) and knockback resistance (0.3) values except for Demon6 which has higher armor toughness

## Spawn Conditions

Most demons spawn using the TestSpawnOniProcedure or TestSpawnOni2Procedure, typically on ground surfaces (SpawnPlacements.Type.ON_GROUND). Specific demons may have unique spawning conditions:
- Muzan: Special spawning condition via MuzanNaturalEntitySpawningConditionProcedure

## General Characteristics

- All demons have sunlight vulnerability (RestrictSunGoal in their AI)
- All demons have similar behavior patterns targeting demon slayers and players
- All demons have MobType.UNDEAD
- Demons drop "Blood of Muzan" item when killed (some entities specifically do this)
- Demons have similar sound effects (generic hurt/death sounds)
- Most demons are immune to knockback from arrows
- Demons can target a variety of entities including players, villagers, and other monsters
