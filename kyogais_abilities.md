Kyogai's gravity manipulation:
Kyogai is immune to his own gravity manipulation, he creates a 30x30x30 block gravity field in front of him (positioned such that he is at the edge of this cubic region). All entities within that region are affected by his gravity field. Any entity that leaves that region will have their gravity reset to normal (down)
If kyogai dies or despawns, then the gravity field is removed
In the following descriptions, whenever the terms up, down, left, right, forward, and backward are used in reference to gravity direction, these directional terms are with respect to the user (the user who is using this blood demon art), so "forward" refers to the direction the user is looking, "backward" is opposite from where the user is looking, "right" is the user's right, "left" is the user's left, "up" is the user's up... and so on. Keep in mind that while the user is immune to their own gravity field created by this blood demon art, they aren't immune to other gravity fields, so if they step into another gravity field, then that affects the user's perspective

Keep in mind that gravity rotations only work if the Gravity Changer mod is installed
If no gravity changer mod is detected, then gravity rotations will be substituted for pushing every entity in the region in the direction of the new rotation, a velocity that will be applied to all entities in the region for 40 ticks

### Right Shoulder

Causes the gravity field to rotate right

- if gravity is down, forward, or backward, then gravity will be set to right
- if gravity is right, then gravity will be set to up
- if gravity is up, then gravity will be set to left
- if gravity is left, then gravity will be set to down

### Left Shoulder

Causes the room he is in to rotate to the left.

- if gravity is down, forward, or backward, then gravity will be set to left
- if gravity is left, then gravity will be set to up
- if gravity is up, then gravity will be set to right
- if gravity is right, then gravity will be set to down

### Right Leg

Causes gravity to rotate forward.

- if gravity is down, left, or right, then gravity is set to forward
- if gravity is forward, then gravity is set to up
- if gravity is up, then gravity is set to backward
- if gravity is backward, then gravity is set to down

### Left Leg

Causes gravity to rotate backwards.

- if gravity is down, left, or right, then gravity is set to backward
- if gravity is backward, then gravity is set to up
- if gravity is up, then gravity is set to forward
- if gravity is forward, then gravity is set to down

### Navel

Creates a 3 claw-like slash attack (5 when he is low health)

- launches a translucent light grey claw model forward, tracing black streaks on the ground (note that the ground depends on direction of gravity, so "the ground" can refer to the wall or the ceiling or the floor, depending on the direction of gravity)
- when the claw is launched, it will immediately drop down in the direction of gravity until it hits a block (ray tracing downwards), and it will draw the black lines on that block face while the claw model travels around the blockface

### Back (self)

Teleports him into another room within his mansion.

- teleports the user to a random safe location within 50 blocks that has at least a 3x3x3 space and has a ceiling (no exposure to sky), the Y level only changing by a maximum of 10 blocks

### Back (target)

Teleports other people into another room within his mansion.

- teleports a living entity on the user's cross hair (line of sight) to a random safe location within 50 blocks that has at least a 3x3x3 space and has a ceiling (no exposure to sky), the Y level only changing by a maximum of 10 blocks

Each time a drum ability is used, it will play a unique animation for each drum, the animation takes 15 ticks (0.75 seconds) to hit the drum, making a unique sound once the drum is hit

For gravity drums, the gravity change will apply 10 ticks (0.5 seconds) after the drum is hit (so 25 ticks after the ability is activated). All living entities that are on the ground and not moving in the direction of the new gravity change by the time the gravity change takes effect will get slammed, suffering damage and also 2 seconds of extreme slowness (and maybe also blindness too, idk yet)

- This means that if a player wants to avoid this attack, then when they hear the drum sound they must jump and move in the direction of the gravity switch, and they will have about 1 second to react

For the claw drum (navel, sometimes misspelled "naval"), the claw attack is spawned as soon as the drum is hit (as soon as the sound occurs, so 15 ticks after the ability is activated), the claw travels forward at a fast rate, and can be dodged like any projectile-based ranged attack

- Players can stay safe from the claw attack by being in the air, as the claw attack only damages those who are on the ground (or standing sideways on a wall, or upside down on a ceiling)

The teleport drum (back drum) does the teleport as soon as the sound is played (so 15 ticks after activation).
